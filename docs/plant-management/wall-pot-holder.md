# Wall Pot Holder

- **Author:** Cameron K. Brooks, FAST Research Group
- **Licence:** MIT (see [LICENSING.md](../../LICENSING.md))
- **Source:** this repository, `cad/wall-pot-holder/`

A wall-mounted grid of tilted pot openings, meant to work with the
[plant pot](plant-pot.md). It is a work in progress, as the source says.

The parameters at the top of `wall-pot-holder.scad` set the grid. The defaults are
5 rows by 3 columns of 55 mm openings, 100 mm deep on 100 mm centres, rotated -45 degrees
about x, with 4 mm walls.
