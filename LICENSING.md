# Licensing

This repository holds designs from several authors under several licences. Each
design keeps the licence its author released it under. Nothing here relicenses
another author's work.

The root [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) (MIT) covers the FAST Research Group's own work: the
designs marked FAST below, the documentation in `docs/`, and the repository's
README. GitHub reads only that file, so it reports the whole repository as MIT. This
table is where the full picture lives.

## What the licences mean here

- **CC BY-SA:** credit the author, and share anything you make from the design
  under the same licence.
- **CC BY-NC-SA** (`ant-trap/`, `irrigation-stake/`): as BY-SA, and **not for
  commercial use**.
- **GNU GPL v2** (`hydroponic-plant-pot/`): changes you distribute stay under the
  GPL v2, with the source and a copy of its text, which is in the design's folder.
- **BSD-2-Clause** (`garden-hose-splitter/`): keep the copyright notice and licence
  text in the design's folder when you redistribute.
- **Public domain and CC0** (`cassava-press/`, `hydroponic-halo-ring/`): no
  conditions.
- **MIT:** keep the copyright notice in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE).

## Notes on the sources

- Where a design is on both Thingiverse and Appropedia, the table gives the licence
  its files were released under on Thingiverse. For five of them (the hose
  splitter, halo ring, pulleys, hand shovel and shovel handle), Appropedia's device
  data lists CERN-OHL-S as the hardware licence, which the Thingiverse release
  doesn't say.
- Thingiverse names the licence without a version for CC BY-SA and CC BY-NC-SA. The
  links above go to version 3.0 of the CC licences. Its "GNU - GPL" link for
  the hydroponic pot points at the GPL v2, with no "or later" grant, and its "BSD
  License" link for the hose splitter points at the 2-clause BSD licence. The hose splitter's release carries no copyright
  line, so its BSD notice names the author as Appropedia does, with the Thingiverse
  handle.
- **Field dressing tool:** Appropedia's page for its file, `File:OSAT2.scad`, gives
  its licence as "Unknown"; the page the file is published on is CC BY-SA 3.0,
  which this table follows.
- **Cassava press:** each of its five `.scad` files' Appropedia file pages gives
  "Public domain", with the author "Unknown".
